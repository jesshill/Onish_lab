# 1 M KPO<sub>4</sub> for NGM Plates

**Date:** 09-26-2022

**Author:** Nishimura lab

---

**Date of preparation:** 

**Prepared by:** 

---

1 M KPO4 buffer (pH 6.0)

To a 1 L flask add:
- 108.3 g KH<sub>2</sub>PO<sub>4</sub>  
- 35.6 g K<sub>2</sub>HPO<sub>4</sub>
- Bring up to 1 L with ddH<sub>2</sub>0  **but dont forget to leave room for pH-ing!
- pH to 6.0 

Autoclave for ~40 min to 1 hr (should be an hour for 1 L volumes)

Store at room temperature.
